Thank you for using One Tap. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the One Tap app for iPhone and iPad handles, where it is stored, and who can see it.

In short

  • One Tap has no account and no server of its own. We never receive your links, notes or browsing history.
  • Your data is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, it is also stored in your private iCloud, which only your Apple Account can read.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising information according to your choices. One Tap Pro removes ads.

Information We Collect#

One Tap is designed to help you save, organize and read your favorite links. The app does not require you to create an account.

The information you save, including categories, link names, URLs, tags, notes, highlights, reminders and offline copies, is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your links or browsing history.

iCloud Sync#

iCloud Sync is optional and off unless you turn it on (One Tap Pro). When it is on, your categories, links, tags, notes, highlights and reminder times are stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so they stay the same on your devices.

This data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it. Offline copies, App Lock and Category Lock settings are never synced.

You can turn sync off at any time in Settings. To remove the synced data, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud and manage the storage used by One Tap.

Link Previews#

When you save a link, One Tap can read the page's title, description, preview image and icon. To do this, the app requests the page directly from that website, without cookies, just as a browser would. No other service receives your links. You can turn link previews off in Settings › Privacy & Data.

The icons in the app's list of popular websites (the suggestions you can add in one tap) are loaded from Google's public favicon service. Google receives the address of that popular website and your device's IP address, as with any web request. Links you save yourself use the icon published by their own website.

Opening Links#

When you open a link, the website sees the visit, as it would in any browser. If the in-app browser is turned off, links open in Safari.

Share Extension, Widgets, Siri, Spotlight and Reminders#

Links saved from the iOS Share Sheet or with Siri and Shortcuts, the data shown in widgets and Quick Actions, Spotlight search results, read-later reminders and the weekly digest are all handled on your device. They are stored in the app's private storage and are never uploaded to us. You can remove links from Spotlight in Settings › Privacy & Data.

Links in a locked category are kept out of widgets, Siri, Spotlight, Quick Actions and notification text.

Offline Copies, Notes and Highlights#

If you save a page for offline reading, a personal copy is stored only on your device and is never shared or synced. Offline copies are excluded from device backups. Notes, highlights and tags you add stay on your device, and in your private iCloud if you turn on iCloud Sync. You can delete offline copies at any time in Settings › Privacy & Data.

Read Aloud#

Read Aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS. The article text is spoken on your device and is not sent to us or to any other service.

App Lock and Category Lock#

App Lock and Category Lock use Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Authentication is performed by iOS. One Tap never receives or stores your biometric data or passcode.

Diagnostics#

iOS provides the app with daily reports about crashes, hangs, launch time and battery use (Apple MetricKit). These reports describe the app's performance, not your content, and never include your links. They are stored only on your device and are sent to us only if you choose to share them from Settings › Privacy & Data. You can delete them there at any time.

If you send us feedback from Settings › Send Feedback, your email includes basic device information (device model, iOS version, language, app version and whether you use Pro) and, if available, a count of recent crashes and hangs. You can review or remove this information before sending.

Backup, Import and Export#

Imports, exports and backups are processed on your device. Exports only leave your device if you choose a destination (for example Files, iCloud Drive or another app) in the share sheet.

How Your Information Is Used#

The information you save is used only to provide the app's features, such as saving, organizing, searching, syncing and reading your links. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Advertising#

The free version of One Tap may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app usage information and advertising-related information, depending on your device settings, consent choices and applicable laws.

Where required, One Tap asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown and requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in Settings › Privacy & Data. Your saved links are never shared with advertisers. Upgrading to One Tap Pro removes ads.

In-App Purchases#

One Tap offers an optional one-time lifetime purchase and an optional yearly subscription to unlock Pro features and remove advertisements. Purchases are processed and verified by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services#

One Tap uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: displays advertisements in the free version.
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): stores your data in your private iCloud when you turn on iCloud Sync.
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit: processes and verifies in-app purchases.

Data Sharing#

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Information may be collected by Google AdMob for advertising in the free version, as described above.

Data Storage and Deletion#

Your saved links and categories remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. If iCloud Sync is on, a copy is also kept in your private iCloud until you remove it as described in the iCloud Sync section.

Children's Privacy#

One Tap is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy#

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact#
