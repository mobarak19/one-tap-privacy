Questions

Do I need an account?

No. One Tap works without sign-up. If you turn on iCloud Sync, it uses the Apple Account you're already signed in with.

Where are my links stored?

On your device. With iCloud Sync on, a copy is also kept in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. We can't see your links. Offline copies and locks always stay on the device.

Can I bring my links from another app?

Yes. Import bookmarks from Safari, Chrome or Firefox (HTML), CSV files, and HTML exports from other read-later apps, with tags, dates and read status. Go to Settings › Import Backup or Bookmarks.

Does One Tap track me?

One Tap itself doesn't. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising information depending on your consent and App Tracking Transparency choices. Your saved links are never shared with advertisers, and One Tap Pro removes ads.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Cancel the yearly plan anytime in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore on the One Tap Pro screen.

Can I read my links offline?

Yes. Save a page for offline reading and it's kept on your device for flights, commutes and bad signal. The free plan keeps 5 offline pages; Pro has no limit.

Does One Tap work with Siri and Shortcuts?

Yes. Say "Save a link to One Tap", or use the Shortcuts app to save links, list unread links or open a random unread one.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The yearly plan starts with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.
